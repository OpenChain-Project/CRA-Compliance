# OpenChain CRA Checklist Case Studies

This folder holds case studies describing how organizations use the OpenChain CRA Compliance Requirements & Checklist in practice. The case studies are authored or approved by the organizations they describe.

Use the [Word case study template](OpenChain_CRA_Case_Study_Template.docx) to prepare a draft. A useful case study identifies the checklist version, explains the organization's use of specific sections, and gives one concrete example of a decision, process, or artifact informed by the checklist. It should distinguish current practice from planned work and link to public supporting material where available.

Before submitting a case study for publication, obtain the organization's approval for its text, attribution, and links. Submit the approved draft through a pull request or contact the working group lead. The project can review it for clarity and evidence before adding it to this folder.

A case study describes an organization's experience. Its inclusion does not establish CRA conformity, certification, or OpenChain endorsement.

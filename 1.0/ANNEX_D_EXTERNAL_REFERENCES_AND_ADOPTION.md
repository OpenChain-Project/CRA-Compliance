# Annex D - External References, Use, Tooling, and Adoption

This living register supports the OpenChain CRA Compliance Requirements & Checklist.

The register records organizations, public resources, tools, products, forums, events, or other references that have indicated that they reference, use, adopt, evaluate, map to, or rely on the OpenChain CRA Compliance Requirements & Checklist in public materials, internal compliance work, customer guidance, tooling, advisory work, or related implementation activities.

Inclusion in this register does not mean that the organization endorses the checklist, certifies the checklist, provides legal approval, or makes any statement of CRA compliance. It also does not create OpenChain membership, sponsorship, certification, audit status, or any obligation to maintain a public website reference.

Organizations may be listed through either of the following routes: a public webpage, blog post, documentation page, solution page, or other public material referencing the OpenChain CRA Compliance Requirements & Checklist; or written confirmation from an authorized company representative that the organization references or uses the checklist and agrees to be listed.

Where no public link is available, OpenChain may record "confirmation on file" instead of publishing private correspondence. Any organization may request correction or removal at any time.

Basis / Status values are evidence categories, not endorsement categories:

* `public-reference` - a public webpage, article, documentation page, or resource references the OpenChain CRA Compliance Requirements & Checklist.
* `written-confirmation` - an authorized representative has confirmed the listing in writing.
* `confirmation-on-file` - confirmation is held by the project but private correspondence is not published.
* `in-use/mapped` - the organization has stated that it uses, maps to, or relies on the checklist in compliance or implementation work.
* `adopted` - the organization has publicly stated or confirmed in writing that it uses the checklist in internal compliance work, customer guidance, product/process mapping, tooling, advisory work, or CRA readiness activities.
* `tooling-reference` - a public tool, product, platform, implementation guide, or workflow references, maps to, or operationalizes the checklist in a CRA, SBOM, vulnerability management, or compliance-readiness context.
* `planned-reference` - a public event or resource is scheduled to reference the checklist but is not yet published.
* `completed-reference` - a public event or resource has occurred or been published and remains available as public evidence.
* `active-contributor` - the organization publicly states that it contributed to or helped shape the checklist.
* `review-interest` - the organization has stated interest in reviewing, following, or contributing feedback to the checklist or working group activity.
